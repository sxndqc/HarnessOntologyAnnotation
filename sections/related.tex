\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{LLMs as annotators.}
Prior work uses LLMs as annotators for text labeling \citep{gilardi2023chatgpt,he2024annollm}. Closely related studies improve annotations by refining guidelines from errors or annotator disagreements \citep{kim2026refining,kim2026diziner}. These studies use flat labeling tasks, mainly named-entity recognition. We extend the approach to complex annotation by refining the whole harness.

\paragraph{Recursive self-improvement.}
LLMs can reflect on feedback to revise their outputs and behavior \citep{madaan2023selfrefine,shinn2023reflexion}. Recursive self-improvement extends such loops to agent code \citep{zelikman2024self}, including the evolution of harness instructions, tools, and workflows \citep{lee2026metaharness,zhang2026self,lin2026agentic}. We use fine-grained annotation errors to guide this evolution.
