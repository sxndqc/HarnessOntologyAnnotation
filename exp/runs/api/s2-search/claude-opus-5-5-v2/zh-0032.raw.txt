I did not call `search_guidelines` for this document. All six sentences are annotated at both levels. These calls were judgement calls, so they are the places most likely to differ from the gold annotation:

- **Aspect of future events:** "将参与" (s1c3) is `performance`. The two "逐步恢复" events (s1h2, s2r) are `activity`.
- **"后" clauses:** In sentence 2 they are `:temporal (after :op1 …)`.
- **"而" in sentence 3:** It is `contrast-91`.
- **"福岛":** It is a `province`. "福岛第一核电厂" in sentence 4 is a named `facility`.
- **"东日本大地震":** It is a named `event` with the 2011 date attached.
- **Names written as nouns:** "处理水" and "核污染水" in sentence 6 are plain nouns. Where they appear as split words in sentences 2 and 3, they are broken into 水 plus 污染-01 or 处理-01.
- **Modal parent in sentence 2:** Everything in the reported content has the state media (s2m) as its conceiver, not the author.
- **"预计":** The expected 40 years (s5x) is `partial-affirmative` under 预计.
- **"目前":** I hung it on `document-creation-time` rather than `present-reference`.
- **Coreference:** The same wastewater, discharge, monitoring, resumption, import, aquatic products, IAEA and the two countries are linked across all sentences. "排放" in sentence 5 is treated as the same event as "排海".
