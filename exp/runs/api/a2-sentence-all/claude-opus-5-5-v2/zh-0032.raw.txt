I made these choices in the graph:

- **Senses and roles:** I looked up 称呼 and 称为 in Chinese PropBank. Each has only one naming sense: ARG0 is the one giving the name, ARG1 the thing named and ARG2 the name. 核废水 is the ARG1 of both verbs, so it is written once and reused (`:ARG1 s6x2`).
- **Linking the two clauses:** Nothing but a comma joins them, so I used `and`. The two clauses do contrast in meaning, so `contrast-91` is a reasonable alternative.
- **Aspect:** I used `habitual` for both verbs, since each describes how a country usually refers to the water. A gold sentence (chinese_umr-0010) also gives 称为 habitual, but that was my judgment call.
- **Concepts:** 核废水, 处理水 and 核污染水 are each one concept, written as the words appear. I left out the quotation marks around the names. The gold data has no other example of 核废水 to compare with.
