UMR annotates each sentence as a rooted graph whose nodes are concepts (for an event the word's lemma with its PropBank sense number, for a named entity its type with a `:name`, and abstract concepts such as `and` or `have-org-role-92`) joined by the numbered roles of the predicate (`:ARG0`, `:ARG1`, ...) and non-participant roles (`:mod`, `:temporal`, `:place`, `:manner`, `:quant`, ...), with an `:aspect` on every event, `:polarity -` for negation and `:refer-person` / `:refer-number` on pronouns, and adds for the whole document the temporal relations among events and times, the modal relations from `author` or another conceiver to every event with a strength such as `full-affirmative`, and the coreference relations `:same-entity`, `:same-event` and `:subset-of`.
