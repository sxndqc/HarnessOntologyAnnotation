**Summary of changes**

1. **Wrong example for possession and belonging verbs.** The instruction told the annotator that such verbs are `-01`. That sentence directly caused the costliest error. I replaced it with a required frame-lookup procedure:
   - look the predicate up as the whole segmented token;
   - list all of its senses;
   - pick the sense whose role descriptions fit the participants;
   - copy that sense's role numbers.

   A schematic belonging-verb relative clause shows the result: owner is `:ARG0`, possessum is `:ARG1`, and the head noun takes the inverse role.
2. **Concept for fused negation.** The concept is now the verb part without the negator, with its own sense. No polarity is written, and `full-negative` goes in the modal triples.
3. **Entity types for periodicals.** Use the most specific type: `newspaper` or `magazine`, and `publication` only when the kind is unknown.
4. **identity-91 temporal rule.** Rewritten with explicit triples:
   - the reputation state is the parent of the anchor: `(state :contained anchor)`;
   - all other events, including the means or reason of the reputation, hang directly under the anchor with `:overlap`.
5. **Aspect of stative verbs.** Stative verbs stay `state` despite 了, 过, 曾经 or a duration.
6. **Unexpressed relations.** The "unexpressed participants" rule now also covers non-core relations such as `:part-of`.
