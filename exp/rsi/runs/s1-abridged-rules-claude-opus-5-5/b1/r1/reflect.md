**Summary of changes.** The edits target the error patterns from the critique that are likely to recur, roughly in order of cost:

1. **Temporal structure (largest loss).**
   - Each sentence now gets one anchor: its matrix event.
   - Every other event in the sentence hangs off that anchor, or off the event it modifies, with `:overlap` by default.
   - Every event node gets a parent, including events inside noun-phrase modifiers.
   - Vague present adverbs no longer get their own `depends-on` triple.
   - Pure copular identity states are left out of the temporal structure.
   - The rule that discouraged same-sentence event–event links is removed.
2. **Classifiers and adverbs.**
   - A numeral plus classifier is now `:quant` plus `:unit`, including the number 1. This replaces the contradicting "drop" rule.
   - Aspectual, time and scope adverbs are nodes.
   - Only modal auxiliaries are dropped.
3. **Arguments and attachment.**
   - Unexpressed participants are not added; this also replaces the zero-pronoun rule.
   - A noun modified by an aspectual verb plus another verb is the argument of the innermost verb.
   - A modifier shared by coordinated nouns goes on each conjunct.
   - A clause that keeps describing a copular predicate noun attaches to that noun.
   - A physical edition is `:part-of` its publication; a medium noun modifying a program is `:medium`.
4. **Aspect.** New rules cover institutional repeated activity (`habitual`), "already + stop" describing the current situation (`state`), and naming or evaluation passives (`performance`).
5. **Concepts.**
   - Prefer a lexical Chinese PropBank frame over a `-91` predicate when one exists.
   - 不过 takes `but-91`.
   - Add `have-degree-91` for superlatives.
   - 由于 and 以…而 take `:reason`.
   - Split coordinated noun compounds into `and`.
6. **Names.** Drop `:wiki`, keep title brackets as their own `:op`s, and mark a place name that modifies a common noun as `:place`.
7. **Coreference.** Do not build chains for incidental place-name modifiers.
