Additions in summary:
- **Quoted headlines.** A headline in quotation marks that is counted by a classifier, or that follows an article noun, becomes a `publication` named entity. Its words are not analysed. This was the largest cost.
- **Chinese adjectives.** Every Chinese adjective, predicative or attributive, must be looked up. A framed one becomes a state event with `:ARG0` the thing described. `have-mod-91` is only for frameless words.
- **Ability nouns and 有.** An ability, right or opportunity noun with a content verb links through `identity-91`, not `:purpose`. 有-06 joins the 有 trap list.
- **Clause + 时.** The clause's event is `:temporal` directly, with no 时 node.
- **Named occasions.** They become `event` + `:name`.
- **如 "for example".** A sentence-initial 如 becomes `have-example-91`.
- **Reported content (temporal).** A date inside reported content does not anchor the reporting verb. An earlier event inside the content is `:before` the reporting verb.
- **Clause-linking `have-reason-91` / `have-purpose-91`.** These no longer get `:aspect` or a modal triple. A subjectless purpose clause re-enters the main subject, and a realised purpose takes the main event's aspect.
- **Coreference target.** Links go to the nearest earlier noun or name mention, not to a pronoun.
