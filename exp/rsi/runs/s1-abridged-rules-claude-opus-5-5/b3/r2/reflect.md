1. **Pronouns for organizations or publications (largest coreference loss).**
   - In the same sentence, such a pronoun is a re-entrancy of the entity's node; no new `person` node.
   - In a later sentence, the pronoun node links `:same-entity` to the entity itself, not to another pronoun.
   - A possessive pronoun on an event noun adds no `:ARG0` by itself.
2. **`:quote` is now mandatory.** It goes on the top content event of saying, claiming and alleging verbs, and of slogans. The expressed speaker (also an organization) is the modal conceiver, never the reporting verb.
3. **Coordinated root.** The first conjunct is the temporal anchor, and the later conjuncts `:overlap` it. Statements about something the text says no longer exists are past (`:before`).
4. **Contradiction between §1 and §5.3 fixed.** A repeated mention with its own noun in the same sentence is a new node with a `:same-entity` triple. Only pronouns and zero mentions are re-entrancies.
5. **Smaller fixes:**
   - Reciprocal relations put both parties under `and`, not `:companion`.
   - A category noun before a name is the concept, not an `:op`.
   - Connectives get no modal triple.
   - Generic nouns and example-list items get no coreference chains.
   - An event noun under an aspectual verb is `process` and re-enters that verb's subject as its agent.
