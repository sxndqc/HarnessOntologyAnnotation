## Summary of changes

These edits fix the five costly patterns in the critique. They keep the rules that worked: titles after 一篇, 该刊, `:part-of`, pronouns, `but-91` and CPB adjectives. To stay under the 30,000-character limit, I deleted some low-value text: the "two levels" overview, the `:mode` line (rare in news), the AMR type list and two redundant aspect examples.

1. **「…」 titles.** A 「…」 is a `publication` only after 一篇/篇 or an article noun (新闻, 文章, 标题). As the direct object of a reporting verb (报导「…」) it is reported content: a full clause with events, arguments, aspect, modal and temporal triples, and `:quote`.
2. **Temporal anchoring.**
   - A named event + 时, and a date inside reported content, get no temporal parent.
   - A 时-clause event is the child of the main event.
   - In `and`-coordination, the time phrase stays on the first conjunct only, and each conjunct hangs from the one before it.
   - 靠 X V is `:overlap`; reason-before-result applies only to 因为/由于.
   - Under `but-91`/`contrast-91`, the `:ARG1` clause is anchored and the `:ARG2` event hangs from it with `:overlap`.
3. **Reported speech.**
   - The speaker conceives its own reporting events: `(author :full-affirmative 刊) (刊 :full-affirmative 指)`.
   - The content events of 报导/指 get `author` only.
   - Every content event carries `:quote`.
   - The 宣称 claim case keeps its rule (top content node gets both `author` and speaker triples).
4. **`identity-91` in a noun phrase.** When it equates a noun with a title or content (新闻「…」, 能力 + V), it gets `:aspect state` and an `author` modal triple. 能力 + V uses `identity-91`, not `:topic`.
5. **Senses and verbal modifiers.**
   - 合成照片 → `照片 :ARG1-of 合成-01`; 握手的照片 → `:mod (握手-01 :aspect process)`.
   - 复制人 → `人 :ARG1-of 复制-01`; 全世界 → `世界 :mod (全)`.
   - 有能力 → `有-06`; 点出 → `点出-01` (look up V+出 compounds).
   - When no CPB sense fits the meaning in context, use `-00` (注销-00).
6. **Small additions.** A 如-example sentence gets a `have-example-91` root. An institution name before a locative noun is `:place`. 外交政策 → `:topic`.
