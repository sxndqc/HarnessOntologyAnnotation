I annotated all 5 sentences. Some choices go against the PropBank entries or are judgement calls on unclear rules. The comparison with the gold may count these against the annotation:

- **闻名-01 (sentence 1):** PropBank makes the famous entity `:ARG0`. I followed the guidelines' "known/famous for X" rule instead and attached it as `:ARG1-of` on 小报. The X clause (报导) is its `:reason`, with 小报 as the agent.
- **富-01 (sentence 5):** I gave it only `:ARG1` (创意), as the guidelines' have-degree-91 example does. I did not add the newspaper as `:ARG0`.
- **Aspect:** 发行 and 报导 (sentence 1), 综合 (sentence 2) and 发行 (sentence 4) are `habitual`, because they are the newspaper's routine practice. 停止 and 继续 are `state`, and 停刊 is `performance`.
- **未经 (sentence 2):** It is 经-01 with `process` aspect and `full-negative`, and the nested 查证 is `partial-affirmative`.
- **Sentence 3:** 曾经 is a `:temporal` node, but I gave it no temporal link of its own. 短暂 is a `:duration` (it could also be a `:manner`), and 自己 is a reference back to the newspaper node.
- **Sentence 4:** I put 停刊 as the anchor, with `(document-creation-time :depends-on s4x)`. I put 发行 after it. 网络版 has no `:part-of`, because the text does not state whose edition it is.
- **Sentence 5:** 其 is a reference back to the newspaper node, used as `:possessor` of 内容. I attached the "in American history" phrase (上 + 历史) to 报纸 rather than to have-degree-91.
- **Clause links and coreference:** I joined the two clauses of sentence 2 with `and`, since there is no conjunction. The coreference links only the newspaper's mentions, chained across all five sentences.
