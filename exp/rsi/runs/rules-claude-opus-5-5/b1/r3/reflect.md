**Summary of changes**

1. **Aspectual-verb temporal rule.** I rewrote it so the aspectual verb is the parent of its complement event, with `:overlap`. The aspectual verb hangs on the sentence anchor; in a contrast or coordination, that anchor is the first clause's event. The "inner event is the anchor" reading now applies only inside a relative clause. No `:before`/`:after` link may join two clauses that both describe the present.
2. **"Famous for X" pattern.** I added it as an explicit rule with the gold direction: the fame state contains the habitual relative-clause event, and the reason event hangs on that habitual event.
3. **Relative-clause states.** I removed them from the list of nodes that get no temporal parent. A new rule says every node with `:aspect` gets a modal parent and a temporal parent. Copular and identity predicates get a modal parent but no temporal parent.
4. **Fused negation.** The nested event's modal parent is `author`, not the negated verb.
5. **Belong-type rolesets.** The rule is now concrete: the owned thing is `:ARG0` and the owner is `:ARG1`, even where the PropBank glosses read the other way round.
6. **Aspect step 2.** It is limited to stopping or completion verbs. Continuation or ongoing action in the present is `activity`.
7. **Alignment.** A word that occurs more than once is aligned at its own occurrence.
