I annotated all 6 sentences. Most choices are judgement calls that I didn't check against the gold data. These are the least certain:

- **Unchecked against gold data:** I looked up the predicates in PropBank but didn't search the guidelines or the gold annotations. So details like the earthquake as a named `event`, the power plant as a named `facility`, and 福岛 as a `province` follow my own reading.
- **Words not in PropBank:** 排海 isn't in Chinese PropBank, so I used `排海-00`.
- **Sentence 3:** I read 而 as a contrast (`contrast-91`). I left the timing of China's resumption under the negated 知道 as `unspecified`.
- **Sentence 5:**
  - 已储存 is a `state` that holds now, anchored with `:overlap` rather than `:depends-on`.
  - 经 12 年 is a `:duration` rather than a `经-01` event.
  - The forecast 需时 40 年 is `partial-affirmative` under 预计.
- **Date 2011 (sentence 4):** I anchored it as `(root :depends-on s4t)`. The guideline wording for absolute dates was ambiguous, so this direction is a guess.
- **Coreference:** Besides the entities (China, Japan, the waste water, the IAEA, the Fukushima plant), I linked three events across sentences as the same event: the discharge, the monitoring and the resumption of imports. These links rest on my judgement that the participants are the same.
