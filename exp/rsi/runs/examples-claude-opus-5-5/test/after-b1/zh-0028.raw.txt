I annotated all 12 sentences and their document-level relations. The only lookups that returned anything were PropBank senses. A gold search for 接口 / HDMI found nothing, and I didn't look anything up in the full guidelines. So the less certain choices below are judgement calls, not rules I checked.

**Concept names that don't match the text exactly**
- **打座 (s5):** I wrote it as `打座-01`, keeping the text's spelling. PropBank only has 打坐-01, so strictly it should be `打座-00`, or `打坐-01` if the gold fixes the typo.
- **现眼 (s10):** this isn't in PropBank, so it's `现眼-00`.
- **Single-word concepts:** I kept 最佳, 中国人, 龙年 and 回家 as one concept each. Splitting them (into 最 + 佳, or person + China) would break the automatic word alignment.
- **Product names:** 三星 B560 and 福韵 B560 are `product` names with two parts each.

**Interpretive choices**
- **Ability verbs (能够, s3):** I gave connecting and facilitating `habitual` aspect with `neutral-affirmative` strength. I could equally have used `state` for the aspect.
- **很有可能 (s7):** this is only modal strength (`partial-affirmative`) on the `identity-91`, not a separate node.
- **得到满足 (s7):** I treated 得到 as a light verb and annotated only `满足-01`. I also didn't make 消费者 an argument of 满足, since PropBank's example puts the needs in ARG2. Instead 消费者 is the possessor of 需求.
- **更 / 更加 (s10):** these are `:degree` on the verbs. The guidelines ask for `have-degree-91` for comparatives, but that is awkward on a verb like 符合.
- **还不赶快…带回家 (s12):** I read this as a command (`:mode imperative`) with no `:polarity -`. I added an unspoken "you" and linked it to 你 in s4.
- **它 / 这 (s1, s2, s4, s6):** these all point to the monitor, so I chained them to 三星 B560 and the B560 / 福韵 series as one entity. HDMI, VGA, 机身, the 福 character and 龙年 are each linked across sentences too.

The riskiest choices are the imperative strength in s12 and the aspect of the ability verbs in s3.
