**Summary of changes.** These edits address the costliest recurring patterns from the critique and leave the parts that worked untouched.

1. **Temporal anchoring of dates and events.**
   - A past date now anchors as `(document-creation-time :before date)`, and the event of that date as `(date :overlap event)`. This replaces "absolute date depends on root" and `:contained`.
   - A purpose clause now overlaps its main event, and an earlier cause is `:before` its main event.
   - 遗留-type earlier events are `:before` the event that uses their result.
   - A sentence that continues the previous narrative (也, 于是, a result of the event just told) may anchor its main event to that earlier event.
2. **Reason and purpose between clauses.** Clause-level 因为/由于…(于是/所以) and 为(了) are now reified as `have-reason-91` / `have-purpose-91`, each with `:aspect state` and a full-affirmative modal triple. `:reason` stays only for 由于 + noun phrase. The invalid `purpose` / `have-condition` modal constants are removed.
3. **V+量 nouns** (发行量, 销量, 产量): `量 :quant-of V`, with the amount given through `have-quant-91 :ARG2 N :unit 份` on 量. The hidden event gets a temporal triple. Quantities measured at different times are not coreferent.
4. **Entity structure.**
   - Edition and format nouns (版面) are the argument and take `:part-of` the publication.
   - 创始人 is `have-org-role-91 :ARG1` with `:ARG3 (人 :ARG0-of 创始-00)`.
   - An appositive common noun + name is `identity-91 :ARG2`.
   - Named people in Chinese are `individual-person`.
   - An unnamed publication mention keeps its Chinese word (刊 :mod 该).
   - The no-invented-re-entrancy rule now also covers purpose clauses and possessors.
5. **Smaller items.**
   - Sentence-initial 不过 is dropped.
   - 1980年代 is written with `:era`.
   - A "how" modifier of an event noun is `:manner`.
   - The habitual rule applies only to main predications; purpose and relative-clause events get process or activity.
