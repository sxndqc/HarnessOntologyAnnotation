I've annotated all six sentences. There were no gold annotations for 排海 or 福岛 to copy, so a few choices are my own judgement calls:

- **排海** isn't in PropBank, so I used `排海-00`.
- **Fukushima** is typed as `province`. The plant in sentence 4 (福岛第一核电厂) is a named `facility`, and the earthquake (东日本大地震) is a named `earthquake`.
- **Plain modifiers:** 持续 and 多轮 in sentence 2, and the 冷却 in 冷却系统 in sentence 4, are modifiers, not events.
- **Future events with 将** (participate, resume, expand) get aspect `performance` with full-affirmative strength.
- **Reported speech:** for 官媒 (sentence 2) and 岸田文雄 (sentence 3) the author affirms the speaker, the speaker affirms the reporting verb, and that verb is the conceiver of the content. The negated 知道 in sentence 3 is `full-negative`.
- **Shared events:** I treated the discharge (排海/排放), the resumption of imports and the import itself as the same events across sentences. The international monitoring counts as the same event in sentences 1 and 2.
