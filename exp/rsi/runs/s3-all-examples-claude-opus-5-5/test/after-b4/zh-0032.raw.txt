- **Future clauses (将):** the guidelines say a second conjunct takes `:overlap`. I used `:after` instead, because these events are clearly still to come (`s1x :after s1x7`, `s2x3 :after s2x34`, `s3x :after s3x3`, `s4x :after s4x9`).
- **Sentence 2:** 持续 is a plain `:mod` on 磋商-01, and 多轮 is `:frequency (轮 :quant 多)`. 在…框架下 is `:place` of 设立-01, and 基于 is attached as `:ARG0-of` 恢复-02.
- **Sentence 3:** 而 is `and`, not a contrast. 恢复 under 不知道 is `full-affirmative` from the author, because only the time is unknown.
- **Sentence 5:** I gave 预计 a `partial-affirmative` link to 需-01. The odd word tokens (，于, ，预计需时, 30万吨) mean some nodes, such as `吨`, may not align to a word.
- **Coreference:** I left out the 中国 that only modifies 官媒, and I did not link the two 参与 events because their participants differ.
