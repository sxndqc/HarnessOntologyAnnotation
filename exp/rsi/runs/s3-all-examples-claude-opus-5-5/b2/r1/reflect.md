**Summary of changes.** The critique's recurring problems are addressed as follows:

1. **Temporal labels.**
   - A date or period of the clause now relates to its event with `:overlap`, not `:contained`.
   - A purpose clause relates to the main event with `:overlap`, and a reason clause with `:before` (or `:overlap` when not earlier). This replaces "purpose is `:after`".
   - A participle describing an earlier state of a noun (遗留下来的) gets `(event :before participle)`.
2. **Anchoring a result sentence.** A sentence that states the result or a feature of the event just narrated (也…成了…特色) anchors to the previous sentence's main event with `:overlap`.
3. **Reified clause skeletons.** 因为…(于是) and 为(了)… that structure the whole sentence become `have-reason-91` / `have-purpose-91`, with `state` aspect, an author modal triple and no temporal triple.
4. **Arguments and relative clauses.**
   - X版面的《Y》 becomes `版面 :part-of Y`.
   - A clause saying what the created thing does becomes `:ARG0-of` on that thing.
   - Appositive nouns are linked through `identity-91`.
   - Verb compounds such as 改为-01 are kept whole.
   - The no-invented-arguments rule is now also in the checklist.
5. **Person nodes.**
   - In Chinese, a named person or a personal pronoun is `individual-person`.
   - A ‑人 agent noun becomes `人 :ARG0-of verb`.
6. **Quantity pattern.** 发行量 becomes `量 :quant-of 发行-01`. The value reached becomes `量 :ARG1-of (have-quant-91 :ARG2 N :unit 份)`.
7. **Smaller items.**
   - `but-91` is used only within one sentence.
   - 1980年代 is `:era 1980`.
   - 黑白印刷 is `:manner`.
   - New aspect rules for purpose verbs, relative clauses describing what a thing does, and 遗留下来的.
   - Examples added; the misleading `but-91` example is fixed.
