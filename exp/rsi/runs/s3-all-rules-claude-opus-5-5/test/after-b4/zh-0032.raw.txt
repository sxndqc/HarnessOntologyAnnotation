All 6 sentences of zh-0032 are annotated above, followed by one combined graph of the temporal, modal and coreference relations.

These choices are judgement calls that a gold annotation could treat differently:
- **Single tokens:** sentence 5 has words written together, such as "，于" and "，预计需时". I still annotated 预计-01 and 需-01 as two separate events.
- **No PropBank entry:** 排海 (discharge to sea) is not in PropBank, so I used 排海-00. The water in quotation marks (处理水, 核污染水) is treated as a plain concept, not a name.
- **Quantities:** I attached the 300,000 tonnes stored to the waste water with have-quant-91.
- **后 (after) clauses:** I made each one a 后 node. I did not check this against the gold data.
- **Contrast and coordination:** I took sentence 6 (Japan calls it X, China calls it Y) as a contrast-91. I took 而 in sentence 3 as a plain "and".
- **Japan's welcome in sentence 2:** I took the arrangement Japan welcomes as asserted by the author, not as an uncertain complement of "welcome".
- **Coreference:**
  - The waste water is linked as one entity across sentences 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6.
  - The discharge to sea is linked as one event across sentences 1, 2, 3 and 5.
  - The resumption of seafood imports is linked as one event across sentences 1, 2 and 3.
  - The international monitoring is linked as one event across sentences 1, 2 and 3.
  - China and Japan are linked whenever they act as participants. I also included some mentions where the country only modifies a noun (日本水产 "Japanese seafood", 日本首相 "Japanese prime minister"), which the abridged rules say not to link across sentences.
