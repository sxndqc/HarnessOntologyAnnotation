I annotated all 12 sentences and the document-level relations. A few choices are my own conventions and may not match the gold data:

- **No gold check:** I looked up the predicates in PropBank, but I did not search the guidelines or the gold annotations before writing.
- **Made-up concepts:** "16:9" is `rate-entity-91` and "1920 × 1080" is a `×` node with two operands.
- **Interface and model names:** D-Sub, VGA and HDMI are named `product` nodes. They are not linked across sentences, since they name kinds of interface.
- **Time:** the text describes a product, so I anchored sentences to the document creation time with `:depends-on`. 2012 and the coming dragon year (sentences 7 and 12) are treated as future. Sentence 9's 设计 (design) is the exception, as a finished act before the present.
- **Modal strength:** 能够 (can) in sentence 3 and 你值得拥有 (you deserve to own it) in sentence 4 are `neutral-affirmative`. 很有可能 (very likely) in sentence 7 is `partial-affirmative`. Sentence 12 is read as a rhetorical command ("why not hurry and bring it home"): imperative, `neutral-affirmative`.
- **Left out:** 看起来 (looks) in sentence 10 has no node.
- **Coreference:** 它 (it) in sentences 1–3 is linked to 三星 B560 (Samsung B560) in sentence 4. That model is linked to 福韵 B560 (Fuyun B560) in sentence 8. Both are linked to 福韵 in sentence 12. The two mentions of the B560 series and of the 福 character are linked, and so are the two mentions of 龙年 (dragon year).
