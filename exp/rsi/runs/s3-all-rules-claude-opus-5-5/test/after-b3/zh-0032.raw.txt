I wrote graphs for all six sentences and one set of document-level relations. I did not check the output against the guidelines, and a few choices are my own calls on unsettled cases:

- **New concepts:** 排海 is not in PropBank, so I used `排海-00` with the wastewater as `:ARG1`. I typed 东日本大地震 as `earthquake`, 福岛 as `province`, the power plant as `facility` and 东京电力公司 as `company`.
- **Modal strength:** Kishida's "不知道" in sentence 3 is `full-negative` for both him and the author. China's resumption of imports under it is `unspecified`. In sentence 5, 需时 40 年 under 预计 is `partial-affirmative`. Events Japan "welcomes" in sentence 2 are plain `full-affirmative`; `neutral-affirmative` would also have been reasonable.
- **Future events:** Events marked with 将 (taking part, resuming imports, putting measures in place) get `performance` aspect and an `:after` temporal link.
- **Sentence 5:** 30万吨 is written as `:quant 300000 :unit 吨` on 核废水 rather than reified with `have-quant-91`. "使用 后" is an `after` time phrase on the wastewater.
- **Coreference:**
  - Linked: the same wastewater across its different names (废水, 核污染水, 处理水, 核废水), the discharge events, resuming imports, and the main China and Japan participants.
  - Not linked: China and Japan where they only modify a noun or appear in the example list.
  - Monitoring is linked only between sentences 1 and 2, not to sentence 3's IAEA monitoring plan.

The tokens "，于" and "，预计需时" in sentence 5 are single tokens in the input. That doesn't affect the graphs, but it may matter when alignment is computed.
