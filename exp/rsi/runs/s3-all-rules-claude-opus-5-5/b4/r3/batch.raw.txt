I annotated all five sentences (16–20) and the document-level relations. A few choices could reasonably go another way:

- **Rule-driven choices:**
  - 「…」 headlines are treated as names. Their words are not analysed.
  - "2000 年 总统大选" is a named event with no time link.
  - Sentence 18 uses have-example-91 for the opening 如.
  - Sentence 19 uses have-reason-91 for the fronted 为了. That follows the guidelines' description of gold; I didn't check it against a gold example.
- **No nodes:** 甚至, 不过 (it opens the sentence) and the additive 也/还 get no nodes.
- **Unsettled cases:**
  - In sentence 16, the guidelines say to give the report's content an author link. I followed that, even though the content is the tabloid's made-up story.
  - In sentence 17 I used `点出-01` because PropBank lists it. In the text, 点 and 出 are two separate words.
  - I used `该刊` and `墙上` as concepts because each is one word in the text.
  - I linked 荒唐 (sentence 17) to the document creation time with `:overlap`. `:depends-on` is also possible for a statement about the present.
  - Sentence 20 treats the 「…」 title as a named publication that the news item is identical to (identity-91), as the guidelines describe for a headline after a news noun.
- **Coreference:** I linked the publication through 该刊 (16), 周报 (17), 它 (18), 他们 (19) and 该刊 (20). Sentences 1–15 weren't provided, so nothing links back to them. I didn't link the generic 外星人 (aliens).
