- **Earlier sentences:** I did not have sentences 1–15, so there are no links to them. The anchor event of each sentence hangs on `document-creation-time`, and coreference covers only these five sentences.
- **Headlines:** in sentence 16 I analysed the 「」 headline as reported content of 报导 (with `:quote`), because it has no classifier. In sentences 18–20 the headlines come with 篇, so I treated them as unanalysed `publication` names. Gold may treat sentence 16's headline as a name too.
- **Aspect of 报导 / 刊登:** I used `performance`, because these are single dated reports. The guidelines' "routine publishing is `habitual`" rule could be read to cover them.
- **该刊:** I split it into `刊` with `:mod 该`, though it is one word.
- **Single-word concepts:** I kept 总统大选, 全世界, 墙上 and 好事者 as single concepts, without breaking them down.
- **注销 in sentence 16:** I used `注销-01` as the text has it, though "published" seems to be the meaning.
- **有能力 in sentence 17:** 点出 is attached as `:mod` of 能力, with `neutral-affirmative` strength under 有. Its `habitual` aspect is my own call.
- **Coreference:** I linked the publication across sentences: 该刊 → 周报 → 它 → 他们 → 该刊. I did not link 外星人 or 美国, since they are generic or appear only inside names.
