二、分数和检验是怎么算的

  • AnCast：用仓库里那份 AnCast++（review-round2/vendor/umrancast，文档级打分，固定随机种子），每个“运行输出、gold”文件对算一次，得到句子级、文档级模态、文档级时间、共指四个 F 分和综合分。一次运行在一组文档上的分数是各篇分数的平均，和上一轮的做法相同。juːmæʧ 一列沿用上一份汇报的数字，放在这里对照。
  • 检验的单位是一次运行。测试集的五个基本设定各 10 次；带 skill 的设定和训练集各 5 次（5 种措辞各 1 次）。
  • 两个设定的比较给三样东西：平均差；Welch 的 95% 区间；双侧精确置换检验的 p 值（把两组运行合在一起，枚举所有同样大小的分法，看有多大比例的分法差得不比实际小）。
  • 多重比较：同一张表、同一列的所有比较一起做 Holm 校正。演变表每列 9 个比较，五个设定的表每列 7 个。
  • 5 对 5 的比较有上限：置换检验能给出的最小 p 是 2/252，约 0.008；乘以 9 之后是 0.071。所以训练集上（都是 5 对 5）任何一个比较校正后都不可能显著，测试集上“第 1 轮对第 2 轮”也是这样。表里黄色的一颗星就是这种情况：方向清楚，但运行次数不够下结论。

三之二、论文最初版本的反思方法（10 月 10 日稿）

  • 做法。按论文 10 月初稿的“一轮改进”：用当前 skill 标注一篇训练文档并打分对齐，逐句找出问题（每轮最多 250 个），由模型提出带锚点的增、改、删，通过准入检查（锚点在 skill 里恰好出现一次；新文字不能和语料有 8 个连续汉字或 5 个连续英语词相同）后写进 skill。4 篇训练文档依次做，每篇 3 轮，共 12 轮；每做完一篇得到一个阶段的 skill（H1 到 H4），各在测试集跑 5 次（5 种措辞各 1 次），H4 另在训练集跑 5 次。
  • 测试集 4 篇合计：AnCast 综合分 39.5（无 skill）→ 40.7 → 41.2 → 42.3 → 42.6。H4 相对无 skill +3.0（95% 区间 +1.4 到 +4.6，p=0.006，Holm 校正后 0.051，刚好不到 0.05）。和方法 1 第 1 轮（42.2）没有差别（+0.4，p=0.50）。juːmæʧ 42.0 → 45.5（+3.5，校正后 p=0.011）。

第 1 篇之后的 skill
- Follow the points below; they were learned from earlier documents.
- Named organizations (TV stations, broadcasters, companies) are typed with the English named-entity category `company` plus :name, never with the common noun 电视台/台. Government regulators/agencies (e.g. CRTC) are `government-organization`; named persons are `individual-person`; titled programs/channels are `broadcast-program`; streets are `road`, malls/buildings `building`. Place names are `country`, `country-region`, `city`, `local-region` with :name (name tokens as segmented in the text, including generic suffixes such as 市/地区). A place inside an organization name is a separate :place entity.
- Concepts use the base verb lemma. Strip the passive 获 (获批 -> 批-01) and the particles/suffixes 于/予/有 (设于, 设有 -> 设, location is ARG3; 售予 -> 售). Verbs like 调往, 迁至, 购自, 购入, 改为 keep their complements. Do not correct typos. Words with no PropBank frame (e.g. 启播, 回放) get sense -00; check PropBank first and use -01 or -02 only when a roleset exists (pick the roleset whose roles fit, e.g. 加入-02). The subject of 启播 is ARG0. Keep the whole verb word as written (改为, 迁至, 购入, 购自, 设有), not a truncated stem. The only exception is 批予, which is split into 批 + 予.
- Copular 是/为 (X is Y) is `identity-91` with :ARG1 theme and :ARG2 identified entity, never 是-01 with ARG0/ARG1. Only a main-clause identity-91 gets :aspect state and modal relations; appositive, embedded, have-degree and title-description identity-91 get no aspect, modal or temporal relation. In a title/description identity-91 the named entity is ARG1 and the description ARG2. Appositives, descriptions and aliases (e.g. parenthetical abbreviations, 姊妹台, occupation of a person) are also identity-91. Adversative 但 (clause-medial) is `but-91` with :ARG1 the whole preceding coordination and :ARG2 the contrasted clause; a sentence-initial 然而 is not a concept, so the clause relation (e.g. have-reason-91) is the root. Causal 因此/令/有见于 use `have-reason-91` (ARG1 result, ARG2 reason). Superlatives/comparatives use `have-degree-91`. Light verbs (进行) are omitted and the nominal (改组, 改革) is the event.
- Decompose compounds: head noun plus :mod (电视节目 -> 节目 :mod 电视; 小部分; 子公司; 东西 as `and`). Distribute coordinated modifiers/possessors over separate head instances. Language names are language entities with :name attached by :medium (also for the language of programs, 以+language/signal/system). 以+tool/money is :instrument; adverbs (亦, 也, 主要, 全面, 重新, 再次, 遂, 曾, 各自, 相互, 一直, 同, 旗下) are :mod on the event and never dropped; adverbs of how an event is carried out (轮流, 集中, 逐渐, 以标清/高清播放) are :manner; 同时 is :temporal.
- Classifiers/measure words after a numeral (条, 间, 项, 种, 个, 套, 件) are a concept attached as :unit of the counted noun, with :quant for the number. Possession is always :possessor (never :poss); scope adjectives like 地区性/全国性 are :mod, while 本地 (local) is :place; the language of content is :medium; a signal or transmission means (讯号, system) is :medium, not :instrument; the origin of a purchase (海外) is :source; containment of a city in a region is :part-of.
- Clock times are time-of-day entity nodes with two-digit minutes (h19m30, h10m00) under :time of a date-entity, never bare values. A habitual broadcast time is rate-entity-91 with :ARG4 date-entity. 每天 is ARG2 temporal-quantity with :quant 1 and :unit 天. 介乎 X 和 Y is `range` with :op1/:op2.
- Aspect: plan/attempt/compete predicates (计划, 竞争) are endeavor; embedded planned or irrealis events under plan/intent/permission predicates are process; completed bounded events are performance; habitual for generic, characteristic or recurring activities of an institution (播放, 广播, 服务, 制作, 购入, 接收), including coordinated ones; a bounded or completed event, and purpose or modifier events in a realized setting, is performance (not process); 增加 or 覆盖 trends and ongoing coverage are state; the phasal event 开始 is performance; 维持 activity.
- Document-level temporal: anchor only the main event of a sentence to DCT; chain co-occurring/coordinated events, complements, purposes and plan contents by :overlap (not :before); a date-entity of year/month granularity that locates an event is :contained of that event (use :overlap for day-level or same-time dates). Put the DCT relation only on the first date of a chain, not on every date. A completed modifier event (自制, 购自) is :before the main event it modifies (e.g. 播放). Temporal and modal relations hold only for event nodes, never for entity nodes. Do not invent event-event ordering. Modal: complements of plan/request/application/permission/intent events are :unspecified from the governing event; negation belongs to the matrix event; do not use :modal-strength in the sentence graph; every event incl. abstract ones gets a modal relation.
- Coreference: repeated mentions of the same entity (company, 该台, city, province) are separate nodes linked by :same-entity across the document, not by reentrancy; repeated event mentions use :same-event; do not merge parent/subsidiary or add loose subset-of links. Use a PropBank sense matching the usage and its role numbering (e.g. 属-02, 追溯-02, 限于-02), and 允许 takes ARG1 permitted entity, ARG2 action.

Do not put 《》 in name tokens. Include all tokens of a company name, including 公司. A city/place prefix that is part of a company or building name (香港亚洲电视, 列治文 mall) stays in the name tokens and is not a separate :place. Do not replace it with a named-entity type, and do not corefer it with the channel.

Stations are type `station` with :name of all tokens as written (黄大仙 站, 深圳 火车站), without the city as :mod. The common noun 人 is the English concept `person`; a person described by a predicate is a `person` node with :ARG0-of the predicate. Lexicalized agent nouns (援助者, 遇难者) stay one concept and are not decomposed into 人 + event. 浸/浸泡-01: the water is ARG2. 提早 is 提早-01 attached as :temporal/ARG1-of with the amount or 大幅 as ARG2.
- Aspectual, sequencing, deictic and focus adverbs (曾, 已经, 都, 随即, 当时, 还是, 首先 as :temporal only if non-sequencing) are not concept nodes; their meaning goes into aspect and document-level relations.

- Part nouns such as 南部/中部/北部 head the phrase and link to the whole by :part-of (city part-of 南部 part-of region), not :mod or :place. A location noun 位置/地点 of an event is linked by :place-of, not :possessor or ARG1-of.
- Sequenced clauses (后, 随即, 尔后) are a `consecutive` coordination; clauses sharing a subject that follow a comma are grouped in a nested `and` under the main coordination, not one flat `and`. 其中 'among them' is include-91 (ARG2 the whole group, ARG1 the subset), not coordination.

- Ordinal+classifier phrases (第二辆车, 第三次, 第二批) are an :ord ordinal-entity with :value and the classifier as :unit of the ordinal-entity, not of the noun; an elided head becomes implicit-argument.

- Members of a group, convoy or organization (workers of a convoy, staff of an organization) are linked by :part-of to the group, not :possessor/:possessor-of.
