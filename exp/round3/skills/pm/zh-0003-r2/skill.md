- Follow the points below; they were learned from earlier documents.
- Named organizations (TV stations, broadcasters, companies) are typed with the English named-entity category `company` plus :name, never with the common noun 电视台/台. Government regulators/agencies (e.g. CRTC) are `government-organization`; named persons are `individual-person`; titled programs/channels are `broadcast-program`; streets are `road`, malls/buildings `building`. Place names are `country`, `country-region`, `city`, `local-region` with :name (name tokens as segmented in the text, including generic suffixes such as 市/地区). A place inside an organization name is a separate :place entity.
- Concepts use the surface word as written: do not expand or normalize 获批 -> 批, 调往, 迁至, 购自 keep their complements). Words with no PropBank frame (e.g. 启播, 自制, 告吹, 调往, 无意, 播出) get sense -01, never -00. Keep the whole verb word as written (改为, 迁至, 购入, 购自, 设有), not a truncated stem. The only exception is 批予, which is split into 批 + 予.
- Copular 是/为 (X is Y) is `identity-91` with :ARG1 theme and :ARG2 identified entity, never 是-01 with ARG0/ARG1. Only a main-clause identity-91 gets :aspect state and modal relations; appositive, embedded, have-degree and title-description identity-91 get no aspect, modal or temporal relation. In a title/description identity-91 the named entity is ARG1 and the description ARG2. Appositives, descriptions and aliases (e.g. parenthetical abbreviations, 姊妹台, occupation of a person) are also identity-91. Adversative 但 (clause-medial) is `but-91` with :ARG1 the whole preceding coordination and :ARG2 the contrasted clause; a sentence-initial 然而 is not a concept, so the clause relation (e.g. have-reason-91) is the root. Causal 因此/令/有见于 use `have-reason-91` (ARG1 result, ARG2 reason). Superlatives/comparatives use `have-degree-91`. Light verbs (进行) are omitted and the nominal (改组, 改革) is the event.
- Decompose compounds: head noun plus :mod (电视节目 -> 节目 :mod 电视; 小部分; 子公司; 东西 as `and`). Distribute coordinated modifiers/possessors over separate head instances. Language names are language entities with :name attached by :medium (also for the language of programs, 以+language/signal/system). 以+tool/money is :instrument; adverbs (亦, 也, 主要, 全面, 重新, 再次, 遂, 曾, 各自, 相互, 一直, 同, 旗下) are :mod on the event, never dropped and not :manner; 同时 is :temporal.
- Classifiers/measure words after a numeral (条, 间, 项, 种, 个, 套, 件) are a concept attached as :unit of the counted noun, with :quant for the number. Possession is always :possessor (never :poss); scope adjectives like 地区性/全国性 are :mod, while 本地 (local) is :place; the language of content is :medium; a signal or transmission means (讯号, system) is :medium, not :instrument; the origin of a purchase (海外) is :source; containment of a city in a region is :part-of.
- Clock times are time-of-day entity nodes like h19m30 under :time of a date-entity, not quoted strings.
- Aspect: plan/attempt/compete predicates (计划, 竞争) are endeavor; embedded planned or irrealis events under plan/intent/permission predicates are process; completed bounded events are performance; habitual for generic, characteristic or recurring activities of an institution (播放, 广播, 服务, 制作, 购入, 接收), including coordinated ones; a bounded or completed event, and purpose or modifier events in a realized setting, is performance (not process); 增加 or 覆盖 trends and ongoing coverage are state; the phasal event 开始 is performance; 维持 activity.
- Document-level temporal: anchor only the main event of a sentence to DCT; chain co-occurring/coordinated events, complements, purposes and plan contents by :overlap (not :before); a date-entity is :overlap with the event it modifies and a past date gets DCT :before date. Do not invent event-event ordering. Modal: complements of plan/request/application/permission/intent events are :unspecified from the governing event; negation belongs to the matrix event; do not use :modal-strength in the sentence graph; every event incl. abstract ones gets a modal relation.
- Coreference: repeated mentions of the same entity (company, 该台, city, province) are separate nodes linked by :same-entity across the document, not by reentrancy; repeated event mentions use :same-event; do not merge parent/subsidiary or add loose subset-of links. Use a PropBank sense matching the usage and its role numbering (e.g. 属-02, 追溯-02, 限于-02), and 允许 takes ARG1 permitted entity, ARG2 action.
- Place types: use the most specific type from the name. A name ending in 省 is `province`; Taiwan, Hong Kong and mainland China (中国大陆) are `country-region`. A road/street inside a city is linked with :place, and so is a studio inside a building or city (not :part-of). Subsidiaries and a channel belonging to an organization are :part-of that organization. Keep 《》 title marks as the first and last name tokens. Do not put generic suffixes such as 公司 in a company name.
- Split a demonstrative from its noun: 该台 is 台 with :mod 该 (never 电视台, and never one fused concept). Do not collapse a multi-word NP into one concept.
- Clauses that are comma- or semicolon-separated and share a subject are a flat top-level `and` (op1..opN), one per clause, none omitted. The shared subject is a re-entrant :ARG0 on later predicates, never :ARG0-of on the first node. A verb that modifies a noun (V 的 N, participial 购自 X, 播放 on X) hangs on that noun by :ARG0-of/:ARG1-of rather than becoming a clause head. Aspectual verbs (开始, 继续) are full events (-01) with the main event as :ARG1 and carry the subject and clause adverbials. 斥资 + amount is an event (ARG1 the acquisition, ARG2 the money). 来自 and 包括 are ordinary predicates (包括-01, not include-91). Every event, including modifier and aspectual events, gets modal and temporal relations.
- Coreference links connect entity concept nodes (company, channel, city, province, 当局 and CRTC), never name nodes. Chain every repeated mention (including 该台, 当局, a 频道 or 卑诗省 in later sentences) to the earlier mention within one chain. A pronoun in the same sentence reuses the node. Do not link 牌照 with a company, and give nominals that are not events (申请, 计划) no same-event link.
