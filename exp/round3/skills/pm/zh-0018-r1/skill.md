- Follow the points below; they were learned from earlier documents.
- Institutions such as an observatory (天文台 as weather body) are the generic `organization` type, with a modifier noun like 气象 as :mod. Named organizations (TV stations, broadcasters, companies) are typed with the English named-entity category `company` plus :name, never with the common noun 电视台/台. Government regulators/agencies (e.g. CRTC) are `government-organization`; the UN, its agencies and international NGOs are `international-organization`; militaries and militant groups are `armed-organization`; 以军-type abbreviations are decomposed into 军队 :possessor-of a `country` with :name (not an organization with :name); named persons are `individual-person`; titled programs/channels are `broadcast-program`; streets are `road`, malls/buildings `building`. Place names are `country`, `country-region`, `city`, `local-region` with :name (name tokens as segmented in the text; keep 市/地区, but a generic suffix already given by the concept, e.g. 县, 村, 镇 as the concept, is not repeated in the name). A place inside an organization name is a separate :place entity.
- Concepts use the base verb lemma. Strip the passive 获 (获批 -> 批-01) and the particles/suffixes 于/予/有 (设于, 设有 -> 设, location is ARG3; 售予 -> 售). Verbs like 调往, 迁至, 购自, 购入, 改为 keep their complements. Do not correct typos. Check PropBank first and use the roleset whose roles fit (e.g. 加入-02, 要求-02 with ARG1 the person asked and ARG2 the requested action). Verbs with no PropBank frame get -00 (遇袭, 葬, 穿着, 不治身亡 kept as one predicate), while verbs with a frame use the fitting -0N sense. Broadcast words such as 启播, 回放 keep -00. The subject of 启播 is ARG0. Keep the whole verb word as written (改为, 迁至, 购入, 购自, 设有), not a truncated stem. The only exception is 批予, which is split into 批 + 予.
- Copular 是/为 (X is Y) is `identity-91` with :ARG1 theme and :ARG2 identified entity, never 是-01 with ARG0/ARG1. Only a main-clause identity-91 gets :aspect state and modal relations; appositive, embedded, have-degree and title-description identity-91 get no aspect, modal or temporal relation. In a title/description identity-91 the named entity is ARG1 and the description ARG2. Appositives, descriptions and aliases (e.g. parenthetical abbreviations, 姊妹台, occupation of a person) are also identity-91. Adversative 但 (clause-medial) is `but-91` with :ARG1 the whole preceding coordination and :ARG2 the contrasted clause; a sentence-initial 然而 is not a concept, so the clause relation (e.g. have-reason-91) is the root. Causal 因此/令/有见于 use `have-reason-91` (ARG1 result, ARG2 reason). The connectives 由于/因为…所以 use `have-reason-91` with ARG1 the effect/result (the 所以 clause) and ARG2 the reason, whatever the surface order; chained reasoning is nested per clause, not flattened into one big `and`. A 因为/因…而 or 因应 phrase inside a clause is a :cause (因应: :reason) adjunct of the event, never ARG0. Clauses contrasted by 而…则 or 则 are `contrast-91` with :ARG1/:ARG2, not `and`. Superlatives/comparatives use `have-degree-91`. Light verbs (进行) are omitted and the nominal (改组, 改革) is the event.
- Decompose compounds: head noun plus :mod (电视节目 -> 节目 :mod 电视; 小部分; 子公司; 东西 as `and`). Distribute coordinated modifiers/possessors over separate head instances. Language names are language entities with :name attached by :medium (also for the language of programs, 以+language/signal/system). 以+tool/money is :instrument; adverbs (亦, 也, 主要, 全面, 重新, 再次, 遂, 各自, 相互, 一直, 同, 旗下) are :mod on the event and never dropped; adverbs of how an event is carried out (轮流, 集中, 逐渐, 以标清/高清播放) are :manner; 同时 is :temporal.
- Classifiers/measure words after a numeral (条, 间, 项, 种, 个, 套, 件) are a concept attached as :unit of the counted noun, with :quant for the number. Possession is always :possessor (never :poss); scope adjectives like 地区性/全国性 are :mod, while 本地 (local) is :place; the language of content is :medium; a signal or transmission means (讯号, system) is :medium, not :instrument; the origin of a purchase (海外) is :source; containment of a city in a region is :part-of.
- Clock times are written HHhMMm with two digits each (19h30m, 10h00m, 23h44m, 00h15m, never h19m30) under :time of a date-entity, never bare values. Weekday and dayperiod values stay source-language words (星期五, 下午, 午夜, 清晨) as concepts of the date-entity, never English; an approximator such as 许 takes the time as :op1. A habitual broadcast time is rate-entity-91 with :ARG4 date-entity. 每天 is ARG2 temporal-quantity with :quant 1 and :unit 天. 介乎 X 和 Y is `range` with :op1/:op2.
- Aspect: plan/attempt/compete predicates (计划, 竞争) are endeavor; embedded planned or irrealis events under plan/intent/permission predicates are process; completed bounded events are performance; habitual for generic, characteristic or recurring activities of an institution (播放, 广播, 服务, 制作, 购入, 接收), including coordinated ones; a bounded or completed event, and purpose or modifier events in a realized setting, is performance (not process); 增加 or 覆盖 trends and ongoing coverage are state; the phasal event 开始 is performance; 排洪 is activity; 维持 and 着-marked resulting states (漂浮, 浸泡, 困) are state; ongoing 浸/水浸/倾泻 are process.
- Document-level temporal: anchor the first/background event of a narrative to DCT (past :before; present still-valid state :overlap) and link later co-occurring events of the same scene to it by :overlap instead of each to DCT; generic, habitual or timeless statements use present-reference :overlap (not DCT); events inside reported speech are anchored to the speech event; a cause or reason event :overlap its main event; era/dynasty phrases framing a statement are :era and get no temporal relation; chain co-occurring/coordinated events, complements, purposes and plan contents by :overlap (not :before); a date-entity (day, month or year) that locates an event is :contained of that event (use :overlap only for clock-time points). Relate each explicit past date-entity to DCT with :before directly; never chain it to the previous sentence's date or event by :overlap. A completed modifier event (自制, 购自) is :before the main event it modifies (e.g. 播放). Temporal and modal relations hold only for event nodes, never for entity nodes. Do not invent event-event ordering. Modal: complements of plan/request/application/permission/intent events are :unspecified from the governing event; negation belongs to the matrix event; do not use :modal-strength in the sentence graph; every event incl. abstract ones gets a modal relation.
- Coreference: repeated mentions of the same entity (company, 该台, city, province) are separate nodes linked by :same-entity across the document, not by reentrancy; repeated event mentions use :same-event; do not merge parent/subsidiary or add loose subset-of links. Use a PropBank sense matching the usage and its role numbering (e.g. 属-02, 追溯-02, 限于-02), and 允许 takes ARG1 permitted entity, ARG2 action.
- Place types: use the most specific type from the name. A name ending in 省 is `province`; Taiwan, mainland China (中国大陆) and large regions such as 加东/加西 are `country-region`; Hong Kong is `city`. A road/street inside a city is linked with :place, and so is a studio inside a building or city (not :part-of). Subsidiaries and a channel belonging to an organization are :part-of that organization. Do not put 《》 in name tokens. Include all tokens of a company name, including 公司. A city/place prefix that is part of a company or building name (香港亚洲电视, 列治文 mall) stays in the name tokens and is not a separate :place. A country such as 中国 in 中国中央电视台 is a separate country with :place.
- Lexicalized compounds and fixed anaphoric forms stay ONE concept (also 各地, 各区, 现存, 惨死, 常用, 略有, 山泥, 桌椅, 重灾区, 副司长, 送院; do not split character by character or split off 各/副/重; 等 after a list is kept as a member). A fixed four-character modifier (贞孝节烈) is ONE :mod concept, not split, and 贞节牌坊 is one concept (not 牌坊 :mod 贞节): 电视台, 该台 (no separate 该 :mod), 数码制式, 定义户, 大部分. Do not split them into head + :mod. Other free multi-word NPs are still decomposed (head noun + :mod). Keep an overt head noun such as 名义 as the concept, with the name under it. Do not replace it with a named-entity type, and do not corefer it with the channel.
- Clauses that are comma- or semicolon-separated and share a subject are a flat top-level plain `and` (op1..opN; never and-91, which is not used for coordination), one per clause, none omitted. A clause that is the consequence of the preceding event nests under that event by :cause-of, with an `and` over the consequences. The shared subject is a re-entrant :ARG0 on later predicates, never :ARG0-of on the first node. A verb that modifies a noun (V 的 N, participial 购自 X, 播放 on X) hangs on that noun by :ARG0-of/:ARG1-of rather than becoming a clause head. Aspectual verbs (开始, 继续) are full events (-01) with the main event as :ARG1 and carry the subject and clause adverbials. 斥资 + amount is an event (ARG1 the acquisition, ARG2 the money). 来自 and 包括 are ordinary predicates (包括-01, not include-91). Every event, including modifier and aspectual events, gets modal and temporal relations.
- Coreference links connect entity concept nodes (company, channel, city, province, 当局 and CRTC), never name nodes. Chain every repeated mention (including 该台, 当局, a 频道 or 卑诗省 in later sentences) to the FIRST mention of the entity in the document (not the most recent one) within one chain; later 牌坊 mentions point to the first 牌坊. A pronoun in the same sentence reuses the node. Do not link 牌照 with a company, and an anaphoric re-mention of an earlier event (申请, 此限制) is a bare concept with no sense, aspect, temporal or modal relation, linked to its antecedent by :same-event. A plain noun 计划 as an argument of another event is also a bare concept.
- Modal: events under 可 (can/possible, e.g. 可接收, 可追溯) get :neutral-affirmative; 难以/难 + V is 难-01 (aspect state) with V as ARG0; the author is full-affirmative on 难, and 难 is the modal source of :partial-negative on V (no polarity -). 无需 is the single predicate 无需-01 (no polarity) and, like 需要, takes the embedded event as sole argument; its subject belongs to the embedded event.
- Special constructions: 联同 joins co-agents as `and` ARG0 (not :companion). 不同 takes one ARG0 `and` of the compared items. Role nouns (股东) are have-org-role-92 (ARG2 the organization); kinship is have-rel-role-92. 以…等语言 is 语言 :ARG2-of include-91 with ARG1 an `and` that includes 等. 继续 and stative 属/讲 relative clauses get :aspect state; 继续 takes only the embedded event as ARG1. 改为 target is ARG2 (change from X is :source). The result of 改组为 is :end-state. A channel that broadcasts is ARG0 of 播放. A channel belongs to its station by :part-of.
- Keep source-language lexemes for relators/modifiers: temporal 后 stays 后 (not 'after'); 至少/总共 are :mod on the noun with a bare numeric :quant; 每 is :quant; non-numeric quantifiers (各, 多, 所有, 大部分) are :mod, not :quant; list-final 等 is not a conjunct; 'X 等' exemplification and sentence-initial 如 use have-example-91; 然后 is expressed by a `consecutive` coordination of the steps; a purpose verb phrase (奏请) is :purpose of its action.
- Era/period words (古代, 清代, 封建时代, X时期) modifying a noun or event are :era, not :temporal/:mod; 内/上/下 after a place noun is a :direction child of that noun; 一般 is :frequency; 亲 is :manner; 最后 is :temporal; a 为 recipient is :affectee; 'one of N X' is X :subset-of the full X set with :quant 1 and the classifier :unit.
- have-org-role-92: holder is ARG1 (person has :ARG1-of), organization ARG2, title ARG3; no :aspect on have-org-role-92 (author modal only). Kinship/social-relation nouns (家属, 同乡) are have-rel-role-92 ARG1-of with ARG2 the reference entity. 得到 is a full event (得到-01) whose ARG1 is the obtained noun/event. Eventive nouns (倡议, 解放, 平等) are events with -01 senses, not :mod.
- Within one sentence a repeated or zero-anaphoric entity reuses the same variable (re-entrancy), never a new node plus :same-entity. Subset/part-whole links (including each member of 'X includes A and B', and later specific instances of an earlier generic noun such as 牌坊) go in document-level coreference as :subset-of, not as in-graph :subset. Chain repeated generic/topic nouns (牌坊, 制度; not 旌表, 守节, 拨发 or other event-type nouns, nor 年间) to their first mention; do not chain country names or different events.
- Aspect: do not default to habitual for generic or procedural descriptions; dynamic events (束缚, 记载, 附, 炫耀, 撰, 建, 立, purpose events like 表扬) are performance, 允许/恪守/平等/掌控 are state, ongoing practice (实行) is activity.
- Document-level temporal relations always have document-creation-time as the FIRST argument: (document-creation-time :before e), (document-creation-time :overlap e), never (e :before document-creation-time). Relation nodes (have-cause-91, but-91, have-example-91) get no temporal or modal relation of their own.
- Event nominals denoting a concrete occurrence (空袭, 袭击, 援助, 救援, 追杀, 事发, 武装, 行驶, 安全, 裁决, 行动) are -01 events with aspect, modal and temporal relations, also as modifiers; 工作人员 is 人员 :ARG0-of 工作-01. Only abstract nouns (旌表, 记载, 建造, 守节 inside a noun phrase) are bare concepts without sense, aspect, temporal or modal relations; their modifiers are :mod, and the about-phrase of 记载/资料 is :topic. Attributive adjectives (有名) are :mod, not events.
- Existential/locative 有 ('in X there are N Y') is 有-03 with ARG0 the place/possessor and ARG1 the thing, not exist-91. A permitted event under 允许 is :neutral-affirmative; the content of a proposal/request (倡议, 要求) is :unspecified under it. Each event is affirmed by the author directly; purpose and relative-clause events get no extra modal edge to other events.
- Within a sentence, repeated mentions of the same entity (e.g. 牌坊) reuse one node; adjacent nouns without a conjunction are head+:mod (对联 诗句); coordinated items sharing a head noun coordinate the modifiers under one head; a shared modifier/possessor attaches once to the `and`.
- Historical or past-era descriptions (events and states located in a past period, e.g. under a dynasty) are (document-creation-time :before e), even when stative; each such event is anchored directly to DCT, not via a date or another event.
- Modifier + common noun phrases (北洋政府, 台金地区, 政府当局) are the head noun with :mod, not a named entity with :name; a monument or building whose name ends in a type noun (贞孝坊, 牌坊) keeps that noun as the concept, with the personal name as a separate individual-person :mod.
- Degree adverbs (非常, 略有, 最 on an adjective such as 早) are :degree; 普遍 is :manner. Locative 位于 + place modifying a noun is a :place relation, not a 位于-01 event.
- Verbal modifiers of a noun (守节, 常用, 相关, 现存 as predicates) are -01 events attached by :ARG0-of/:ARG1-of with :aspect state, not :mod; coordinated ones are joined by `and`.
- Frequency 'once per N years' / 每年 is rate-entity-91 (ARG1 1, ARG3 temporal-quantity with :quant/:unit) under :frequency; 每小时 is :frequency, not :mod. A numeral+time unit (12小时) is a temporal-quantity (:quant, :unit) attached by :duration, not a fused concept.
- Temporal delimiters (直至, 起, 内 'within') are reified concepts under :temporal with the time expression/quantity as :op1, and they are the anchors in the document-level temporal relations.
- Event compounds made of a verb morpheme plus its noun argument (水浸, 山泥倾泻) are decomposed into the event (浸-01, 倾泻-01) with the noun as its argument, and get aspect, modal and temporal relations.
- Document-level: an explicit date-entity is related to DCT (DCT :before date) and the event it locates is :contained in it, without a separate DCT relation for that event. Habitual/generic events overlap DCT. For quoted or reported content, the speaker entity (ARG0 of the saying verb or the quoted person node) is the modal source for the embedded events, and the author is :full-affirmative toward that speaker.
- Every modifier, unit, degree word, manner word and date-entity filler (:mod, :unit, :degree, :manner, :quant words like 所有/几乎/多, :weekday, :dayperiod, :time such as 05h30m) is its own concept node with a variable, never a bare string. Only numbers, names, :aspect, :polarity and similar true constants stay bare. Approximators (约) are a node with the number as :op1; 每小时 is one concept under :frequency.
- Record/superlative 'new high since records began' is have-degree-91 (ARG2 the quality, ARG3 the degree word) on the measured entity, not a separate 创下 event. 有记录以来 is 以来 with :op1 记录 (concept kept) which is ARG2-of exist-91 (ARG1 the place); a given start date goes under have-start-91. 首次 and 第N次 are an :ord ordinal-entity with only :value (never a :unit 次); an event count like 两次/3次 is have-frequency-91 (ARG2 count) or :frequency.
- The single argument of stative/adjectival predicates (大, 低, 严重, 突然) is ARG0. Recurring-frequency events (五百年一遇) are aspect habitual, anchored to present-reference, and take the described thing as a re-entrant ARG1. Bounded events such as 启动, 停课, 加大, 关闭 are performance.
- 当地 is linked by :same-entity to the city mentioned earlier. Do not chain repeated event/weather nouns or predicates with different roles (暴雨, 遇, 浸, 停课, 学校). 政府/当局/港府 mentions of the same government body are chained together.
- Named typhoons are `natural-disaster` with :name containing all tokens (台风 苏拉); a quoted nickname (黑雨) is also a natural-disaster with :name; a later common noun (风暴) is identity-91. 港府 is `government-organization` with :name. Stations are type `station` with :name of all tokens as written (黄大仙 站, 深圳 火车站), without the city as :mod. Common nouns for people keep the source word 人, not `person`. 浸/浸泡-01: the water is ARG2. 提早 is 提早-01 attached as :temporal/ARG1-of with the amount or 大幅 as ARG2.
- Aspectual, sequencing, deictic and focus adverbs (曾, 已经, 都, 随即, 当时, 还是, 首先 as :temporal only if non-sequencing) are not concept nodes; their meaning goes into aspect and document-level relations.
- A demonstrative with or without classifier (该, 这次, 本次, 这场) is a :mod concept. 'X事件' keeps 事件 as head, identity-91 with the event X-01.
- Part nouns such as 南部/中部/北部 head the phrase and link to the whole by :part-of (city part-of 南部 part-of region), not :mod or :place. A location noun 位置/地点 of an event is linked by :place-of, not :possessor or ARG1-of.
- Sequenced clauses (后, 随即, 尔后) are a `consecutive` coordination; clauses sharing a subject that follow a comma are grouped in a nested `and` under the main coordination, not one flat `and`. 其中 'among them' is include-91 (ARG2 the whole group, ARG1 the subset), not coordination.
- 根据 X (的说法) is have-source-91 with ARG2 the source, not a 根据-01 event. 简称 is 简称-01 (aspect state) with ARG1 the entity and ARG2 the abbreviation name. 发动 + event noun and 遭到 + event are events whose ARG1 is the nested event.
- Distance/extent: the measured quantity of 长达, 相隔/隔 and 离 is a core argument (not :extent); 离 uses 离-02 (ARG0 thing, ARG1 reference, ARG2 distance); 仅 is :mod on the quantity. 'N units before X' is a temporal-quantity with :mod 前 (:op1 X) attached by :temporal.
- Aspect: ongoing 正在 events, 封锁, 行驶, 逃跑, 前往, 运送, 存放 and 继续 are activity; 逃命 is endeavor; 保证, 安全, 致力于 and identity-91 are state; bounded completed or requested events (进入, 抢救, 服役, 道歉) are performance. Every event node gets aspect, modal and temporal relations; abstract predicates (have-cause-91, include-91) do not.
