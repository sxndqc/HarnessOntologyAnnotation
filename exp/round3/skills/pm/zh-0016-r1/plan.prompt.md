You are improving an annotator of Uniform Meaning Representation (UMR). The annotator is a language model that gets a short description of UMR, two lookup tools (the full UMR guidelines and PropBank), a list of instructions (its "parse skill", below) and a whole document, and writes every sentence's graph and the document-level temporal, modal and coreference relations.

Below are the parse skill in full and the problems found when the annotator's last annotation of a document was compared with the gold, grouped by evaluator check.

Return edits to the parse skill. Each edit is an object with:
- "action": "add" (insert a missing rule, example or common error after the anchor), "modify" (replace an instruction, common error or example that is wrong or too narrow: the anchor is replaced by the text) or "delete" (remove text that causes an error or contradicts the gold: the anchor is removed)
- "anchor": text copied exactly from the parse skill that occurs in it exactly once (for "add" it may be "", and the text then goes to the end)
- "text": the new text (a bullet point starting with "- "; empty for "delete")

Answer with a JSON object {"edits": [...], "suggestions": [...]} and nothing else; "suggestions" are changes outside the parse skill (to the tools, to the procedure) that you think are needed; they are recorded, not applied.


## Parse skill

- Follow the points below; they were learned from earlier documents.
- Named organizations (TV stations, broadcasters, companies) are typed with the English named-entity category `company` plus :name, never with the common noun 电视台/台. Government regulators/agencies (e.g. CRTC) are `government-organization`; named persons are `individual-person`; titled programs/channels are `broadcast-program`; streets are `road`, malls/buildings `building`. Place names are `country`, `country-region`, `city`, `local-region` with :name (name tokens as segmented in the text; keep 市/地区, but a generic suffix already given by the concept, e.g. 县, 村, 镇 as the concept, is not repeated in the name). A place inside an organization name is a separate :place entity.
- Concepts use the base verb lemma. Strip the passive 获 (获批 -> 批-01) and the particles/suffixes 于/予/有 (设于, 设有 -> 设, location is ARG3; 售予 -> 售). Verbs like 调往, 迁至, 购自, 购入, 改为 keep their complements. Do not correct typos. Check PropBank first and use the roleset whose roles fit (e.g. 加入-02, 要求-02 with ARG1 the person asked and ARG2 the requested action). Verbs with no frame (守节, 惨死, 查访, 上报) get -01; only broadcast words such as 启播, 回放 keep -00. The subject of 启播 is ARG0. Keep the whole verb word as written (改为, 迁至, 购入, 购自, 设有), not a truncated stem. The only exception is 批予, which is split into 批 + 予.
- Copular 是/为 (X is Y) is `identity-91` with :ARG1 theme and :ARG2 identified entity, never 是-01 with ARG0/ARG1. Only a main-clause identity-91 gets :aspect state and modal relations; appositive, embedded, have-degree and title-description identity-91 get no aspect, modal or temporal relation. In a title/description identity-91 the named entity is ARG1 and the description ARG2. Appositives, descriptions and aliases (e.g. parenthetical abbreviations, 姊妹台, occupation of a person) are also identity-91. Adversative 但 (clause-medial) is `but-91` with :ARG1 the whole preceding coordination and :ARG2 the contrasted clause; a sentence-initial 然而 is not a concept, so the clause relation (e.g. have-reason-91) is the root. Causal 因此/令/有见于 use `have-reason-91` (ARG1 result, ARG2 reason). The paired connectives 由于/因为…所以 use `have-cause-91` with ARG1 the effect/result (the 所以 clause) and ARG2 the cause/reason, whatever the surface order. A 因为/因…而 or 因应 phrase inside a clause is a :cause (因应: :reason) adjunct of the event, never ARG0. Clauses contrasted by 而…则, 则 or 并未 are `and-contrast`, not `and`. Superlatives/comparatives use `have-degree-91`. Light verbs (进行) are omitted and the nominal (改组, 改革) is the event.
- Decompose compounds: head noun plus :mod (电视节目 -> 节目 :mod 电视; 小部分; 子公司; 东西 as `and`). Distribute coordinated modifiers/possessors over separate head instances. Language names are language entities with :name attached by :medium (also for the language of programs, 以+language/signal/system). 以+tool/money is :instrument; adverbs (亦, 也, 主要, 全面, 重新, 再次, 遂, 曾, 各自, 相互, 一直, 同, 旗下) are :mod on the event and never dropped; adverbs of how an event is carried out (轮流, 集中, 逐渐, 以标清/高清播放) are :manner; 同时 is :temporal.
- Classifiers/measure words after a numeral (条, 间, 项, 种, 个, 套, 件) are a concept attached as :unit of the counted noun, with :quant for the number. Possession is always :possessor (never :poss); scope adjectives like 地区性/全国性 are :mod, while 本地 (local) is :place; the language of content is :medium; a signal or transmission means (讯号, system) is :medium, not :instrument; the origin of a purchase (海外) is :source; containment of a city in a region is :part-of.
- Clock times are time-of-day entity nodes with two-digit minutes (h19m30, h10m00) under :time of a date-entity, never bare values. A habitual broadcast time is rate-entity-91 with :ARG4 date-entity. 每天 is ARG2 temporal-quantity with :quant 1 and :unit 天. 介乎 X 和 Y is `range` with :op1/:op2.
- Aspect: plan/attempt/compete predicates (计划, 竞争) are endeavor; embedded planned or irrealis events under plan/intent/permission predicates are process; completed bounded events are performance; habitual for generic, characteristic or recurring activities of an institution (播放, 广播, 服务, 制作, 购入, 接收), including coordinated ones; a bounded or completed event, and purpose or modifier events in a realized setting, is performance (not process); 增加 or 覆盖 trends and ongoing coverage are state; the phasal event 开始 is performance; 维持 activity.
- Document-level temporal: anchor every main-clause event/state directly to DCT (past or completed :before; present, generic or still-valid state :overlap) instead of chaining it to other events or to the previous sentence; a cause or reason event :overlap its main event; era/dynasty phrases framing a statement are :era and get no temporal relation; chain co-occurring/coordinated events, complements, purposes and plan contents by :overlap (not :before); a date-entity of year/month granularity that locates an event is :contained of that event (use :overlap for day-level or same-time dates). Put the DCT relation only on the first date of a chain, not on every date. A completed modifier event (自制, 购自) is :before the main event it modifies (e.g. 播放). Temporal and modal relations hold only for event nodes, never for entity nodes. Do not invent event-event ordering. Modal: complements of plan/request/application/permission/intent events are :unspecified from the governing event; negation belongs to the matrix event; do not use :modal-strength in the sentence graph; every event incl. abstract ones gets a modal relation.
- Coreference: repeated mentions of the same entity (company, 该台, city, province) are separate nodes linked by :same-entity across the document, not by reentrancy; repeated event mentions use :same-event; do not merge parent/subsidiary or add loose subset-of links. Use a PropBank sense matching the usage and its role numbering (e.g. 属-02, 追溯-02, 限于-02), and 允许 takes ARG1 permitted entity, ARG2 action.
- Place types: use the most specific type from the name. A name ending in 省 is `province`; Taiwan, mainland China (中国大陆) and large regions such as 加东/加西 are `country-region`; Hong Kong is `city`. A road/street inside a city is linked with :place, and so is a studio inside a building or city (not :part-of). Subsidiaries and a channel belonging to an organization are :part-of that organization. Do not put 《》 in name tokens. Include all tokens of a company name, including 公司. A city/place prefix that is part of a company or building name (香港亚洲电视, 列治文 mall) stays in the name tokens and is not a separate :place. A country such as 中国 in 中国中央电视台 is a separate country with :place.
- Lexicalized compounds and fixed anaphoric forms stay ONE concept (also 各地, 现存, 惨死, 常用, 略有; do not split character by character). A fixed four-character modifier (贞孝节烈) is ONE :mod concept, not split, and 贞节牌坊 is one concept (not 牌坊 :mod 贞节): 电视台, 该台 (no separate 该 :mod), 数码制式, 定义户, 大部分. Do not split them into head + :mod. Other free multi-word NPs are still decomposed (head noun + :mod). Keep an overt head noun such as 名义 as the concept, with the name under it. Do not replace it with a named-entity type, and do not corefer it with the channel.
- Clauses that are comma- or semicolon-separated and share a subject are a flat top-level `and` (op1..opN), one per clause, none omitted. The shared subject is a re-entrant :ARG0 on later predicates, never :ARG0-of on the first node. A verb that modifies a noun (V 的 N, participial 购自 X, 播放 on X) hangs on that noun by :ARG0-of/:ARG1-of rather than becoming a clause head. Aspectual verbs (开始, 继续) are full events (-01) with the main event as :ARG1 and carry the subject and clause adverbials. 斥资 + amount is an event (ARG1 the acquisition, ARG2 the money). 来自 and 包括 are ordinary predicates (包括-01, not include-91). Every event, including modifier and aspectual events, gets modal and temporal relations.
- Coreference links connect entity concept nodes (company, channel, city, province, 当局 and CRTC), never name nodes. Chain every repeated mention (including 该台, 当局, a 频道 or 卑诗省 in later sentences) to the FIRST mention of the entity in the document (not the most recent one) within one chain; later 牌坊 mentions point to the first 牌坊. A pronoun in the same sentence reuses the node. Do not link 牌照 with a company, and an anaphoric re-mention of an earlier event (申请, 此限制) is a bare concept with no sense, aspect, temporal or modal relation, linked to its antecedent by :same-event. A plain noun 计划 as an argument of another event is also a bare concept.
- Modal: events under 可 (can/possible, e.g. 可接收, 可追溯) get :neutral-affirmative; 难以 is :partial-negative, not polarity -.
- Special constructions: 联同 joins co-agents as `and` ARG0 (not :companion). 不同 takes one ARG0 `and` of the compared items. Role nouns (股东) are have-org-role-92 (ARG2 the organization); kinship is have-rel-role-92. 以…等语言 is 语言 :ARG2-of include-91 with ARG1 an `and` that includes 等. 继续 and stative 属/讲 relative clauses get :aspect state; 继续 takes only the embedded event as ARG1. 改为 target is ARG2 (change from X is :source). The result of 改组为 is :end-state. A channel that broadcasts is ARG0 of 播放. A channel belongs to its station by :part-of.
- Keep source-language lexemes for relators/modifiers: temporal 后 stays 后 (not 'after'); 至少/总共 are :mod on the noun with a bare numeric :quant; 各/每 are :quant; list-final 等 is not a conjunct; 'X 等' exemplification and sentence-initial 如 use have-example-91; 然后 is expressed by a `consecutive` coordination of the steps; a purpose verb phrase (奏请) is :purpose of its action.
- Era/period words (古代, 清代, 封建时代, X时期) modifying a noun or event are :era, not :temporal/:mod; 内/上/下 after a place noun is a :direction child of that noun; 一般 is :frequency; 亲 is :manner; 最后 is :temporal; a 为 recipient is :affectee; 'one of N X' is X :subset-of the full X set with :quant 1 and the classifier :unit.
- have-org-role-92: holder is ARG1 (person has :ARG1-of), organization ARG2, title ARG3; :aspect state and an author modal. Kinship/social-relation nouns (家属, 同乡) are have-rel-role-92 ARG1-of with ARG2 the reference entity. 得到 is a full event (得到-01) whose ARG1 is the obtained noun/event. Eventive nouns (倡议, 解放, 平等) are events with -01 senses, not :mod.
- Within one sentence a repeated or zero-anaphoric entity reuses the same variable (re-entrancy), never a new node plus :same-entity. Subset/part-whole links (including each member of 'X includes A and B', and later specific instances of an earlier generic noun such as 牌坊) go in document-level coreference as :subset-of, not as in-graph :subset. Chain repeated generic/topic nouns (牌坊, 制度; not 旌表, 守节, 拨发 or other event-type nouns, nor 年间) to their first mention; do not chain country names or different events.
- Aspect: do not default to habitual for generic or procedural descriptions; dynamic events (束缚, 记载, 附, 炫耀, 撰, 建, 立, purpose events like 表扬) are performance, 允许/恪守/平等/掌控 are state, ongoing practice (实行) is activity.
- Document-level temporal relations always have document-creation-time as the FIRST argument: (document-creation-time :before e), (document-creation-time :overlap e), never (e :before document-creation-time). Relation nodes (have-cause-91, but-91, have-example-91) get no temporal or modal relation of their own.
- Abstract or eventive nouns used as plain nouns (旌表, 记载, 保证, 建造, 守节 inside a noun phrase) are concepts without sense, aspect, temporal or modal relations; their modifiers are :mod, and the about-phrase of 记载/资料 is :topic. Attributive adjectives (有名) are :mod, not events.
- Existential/locative 有 ('in X there are N Y') is 有-03 with ARG0 the place/possessor and ARG1 the thing, not exist-91. A permitted event under 允许 is :neutral-affirmative; the content of a proposal/request (倡议, 要求) is :unspecified under it. Each event is affirmed by the author directly; purpose and relative-clause events get no extra modal edge to other events.
- Within a sentence, repeated mentions of the same entity (e.g. 牌坊) reuse one node; adjacent nouns without a conjunction are head+:mod (对联 诗句); coordinated items sharing a head noun coordinate the modifiers under one head; a shared modifier/possessor attaches once to the `and`.
- Historical or past-era descriptions (events and states located in a past period, e.g. under a dynasty) are (document-creation-time :before e), even when stative; each such event is anchored directly to DCT, not via a date or another event.
- Modifier + common noun phrases (北洋政府, 台金地区, 政府当局) are the head noun with :mod, not a named entity with :name; a monument or building whose name ends in a type noun (贞孝坊, 牌坊) keeps that noun as the concept, with the personal name as a separate individual-person :mod.
- Degree adverbs (非常, 略有, 最 on an adjective such as 早) are :degree; 普遍 is :manner. Locative 位于 + place modifying a noun is a :place relation, not a 位于-01 event.
- Verbal modifiers of a noun (守节, 常用, 相关, 现存 as predicates) are -01 events attached by :ARG0-of/:ARG1-of with :aspect state, not :mod; coordinated ones are joined by `and`.
